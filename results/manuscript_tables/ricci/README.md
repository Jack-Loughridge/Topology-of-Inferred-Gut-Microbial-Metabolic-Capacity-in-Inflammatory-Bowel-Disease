# Ricci manuscript tables

`ibd_feature_block_ablation.tex` is the production-generated table comparing
`B only`, `K0 only`, and the original combined `[B|K0]` classifier at the
prespecified `C=0.02`. Values are mean plus or minus sample SD across the 20
pooled out-of-fold repetitions. The table reports both sample-level and
participant-level performance.

Machine-readable source data and the raw-evidence audit are stored under
`results/figure_source_data/ricci/` and `results/provenance/ricci/`.
